Research

Featured paper

Fresh Produce Prices and Mental Health

Using county-level retail scanner data and repeated cross-sections from BRFSS, this paper instruments produce prices with weather-driven supply shocks in major growing regions interacted with distance-based exposure measures. Higher produce prices significantly worsen population mental health, with larger effects in counties with limited baseline food access.
